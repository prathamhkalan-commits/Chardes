❓ How to Play

1. Choose your name and category.

2. Hold your Android phone horizontally against your forehead.

3. Tilt DOWN when your team guesses the word correctly.

4. Tilt UP to pass.

5. You have 60 seconds to score as many as possible.

Buttons are available as a fallback if motion sensors don't work.
