❓ How to Play

1. Enter your player name and choose a category.

2. Choose 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 minutes, or use Custom.

3. Hold the phone VERTICALLY against your forehead.

4. Keep it roughly upright while reading the word.

5. Tilt LEFT to PASS.

6. Tilt RIGHT to CORRECT.

7. Return close to upright before the next gesture.

Buttons remain available if motion sensors are unavailable.
